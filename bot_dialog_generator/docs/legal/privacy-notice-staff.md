# Privacy notice for studio users

**Effective date:** [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

This notice is for employees and contractors of **[[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]]** who sign in to the Bot_Dialog_Generator studio to design, test or publish assistants.

## Who is responsible

[[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]], [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]], tax ID [[TAX_ID]]. Privacy contact: [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] · [[DPO_CONTACT]]

## What we process about you

| Information | Example | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Your work identity | User ID, name and work email from Microsoft Entra ID | Entra ID, when you sign in |
| Your Entra ID group memberships that are assigned to the studio | "Legal editors" | Entra ID, managed by IT |
| Your access in each area | Owner of Legal, analyst in Customer service | Area owners and platform administrators |
| A record of sensitive actions you take | "Published version 4 of HR helpdesk on 7 Oct 2026 at 10:42" | The studio, automatically |
| Content you create | Bot flows, messages, draft versions | You |
| Technical data | IP address, browser, time of requests | Your browser, our hosting provider |

The studio sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. Your sign-in token is kept only for the browser tab you signed in from (session storage) and is discarded when you close the tab.

## Why we use it

| Purpose | Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR) | Colombia (Ley 1581) |
|---|---|---|
| Sign you in and show you only the areas you belong to | Employment or service contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Your authorization, given as part of your employment relationship |
| Keep an audit log of publishing, access changes and administrator access, to protect each area's privacy and investigate incidents | Legitimate interest in security and accountability (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Same |
| Credit and track the content you author (versions, who published what) | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Same |

[[CONFIRM WITH HR/LEGAL: The audit log is not used to evaluate individual performance. It is used only for security, compliance and incident investigation.]]

## Who can see it

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| Members of an area you belong to | Your user ID and role in that area |
| Owners of that area | The same, and they can change your role or remove you |
| Platform administrators | All areas, memberships and the audit log. Their own access is also logged |
| IT | Your Entra ID groups, which decide your access |
| Our hosting provider, [[HOSTING_PROVIDER_AND_REGION]] | Stores the data on our behalf |

Members of other areas cannot see that you belong to an area they are not part of.

## How long we keep it

| Information | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Your access to an area | Until you are removed, or your Entra ID account or group membership ends |
| Audit log entries | [[RETENTION_AUDIT]] |
| Content you created | As long as the area keeps the bot; it belongs to [[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]], not to you personally |
| Technical logs | [[RETENTION_LOGS]] |
