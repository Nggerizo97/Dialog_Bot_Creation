# Privacy notice for chat conversations

**Effective date:** [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

This notice explains what happens to the information you share when you chat with an assistant operated by **[[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]]** ("we", "us").

## Who is responsible

[[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]], [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]], tax ID [[TAX_ID]], is responsible for your information (the "data controller", or *responsable del tratamiento* in Colombia).

Privacy questions and requests: [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]] · [[PHONE]] · [[DPO_CONTACT]]

## You are chatting with an automated assistant

The assistant is software, not a person. It follows conversation flows designed by our teams[, and it can use artificial intelligence to answer from our own documents]. You can ask to talk to a person wherever the chat offers that option.

**Please don't share sensitive information in the chat**, such as passwords, card numbers, health information or identity documents, unless the assistant explicitly asks for it as part of a secure process.

## What we collect

| Information | Example | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The messages you type and the options you choose | "How many vacation days do I have?" | You |
| A random conversation ID | `usr-k3x9q2a` | Created by the chat in your browser |
| Technical data needed to deliver the chat | IP address, browser type, time of each message | Your browser, our hosting provider |

We do not use the chat to track you across other websites, and the chat contains no advertising cookies or analytics scripts.

| Purpose | Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR) | Colombia (Ley 1581) |
|---|---|---|

| Hand the conversation to a person when you ask | Same as above | Same |
| Keep the service secure and investigate reported faults | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Your authorization |
| [[OPTIONAL: Improve our conversation flows using aggregated statistics]] | [[Legitimate interest]] | [[Your authorization]] |

We do not sell your information. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing.

## Storage in your browser

The chat keeps the conversation in your browser's *session storage* so it survives if you reload the page. It is deleted automatically when you close the tab. It contains only the conversation ID and the messages shown on screen. This storage is strictly necessary for the chat you started, so we do not ask for separate consent for it. The chat sets no cookies.

## Who can see your conversation

- **The team at [[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]] that runs this assistant.** Each assistant belongs to one team, and other teams cannot see its conversations.
- **Platform administrators, only to investigate a reported production problem.** Each access needs a stated reason, is limited in time and to the conversations involved, and is recorded in an audit log.
- **Service providers acting on our instructions:** our hosting provider, [[HOSTING_PROVIDER_AND_REGION]][; and our AI model provider, [[AI_PROVIDER_AND_REGION]], which processes the text of your messages to produce an answer and does not use it to train its models].
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.
