## 1. There are no charges today

- **People who chat with an assistant pay nothing.** Chatting is free, there is no subscription, and no assistant built on Bot_Dialog_Generator takes payments or asks for card details. If an assistant ever asks you for a payment or card number in the chat, do not provide it, and report it to [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]].
- **Staff pay nothing.** The studio is an internal tool of [[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]], with no fees, tiers or usage charges for its users. Internal cost allocation between areas (for example AI usage per area) is an accounting matter, not a charge to people.

Because nothing is sold, there is nothing to refund, cancel or withdraw from today.

## 2. If an assistant offers a paid product or service

Some assistants may in future help you buy something from [[COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME]] (for example, start a product application or book a paid service). When that happens:

1. **Full price first.** Before you commit, you see the total price, including taxes, fees and any recurring charges, and the frequency and length of any subscription. No fee is added afterwards. [[CONFIRM: list any fee types that can apply.]]

4. **Right of withdrawal.** For purchases made remotely you may withdraw within **[[WITHDRAWAL_DAYS — 5 business days in Colombia (Ley 1480, Art. 47); 14 days in the EU]]** without giving a reason, except where the law excludes it (for example, services fully performed with your consent). Write to [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] or use [[CANCELLATION_CHANNEL]].
5. **Refunds** are paid to the original payment method within **[[REFUND_DAYS — at most 30 calendar days in Colombia; 14 days in the EU]]** of the withdrawal or of an accepted claim, with no deduction other than what the law allows.
6. **Reversal of payment (Colombia, Ley 1480, Art. 51).** If you paid by card or another electronic means and the purchase was unauthorized, fraudulent, not delivered or not as described, you may ask your bank to reverse the payment within 5 business days of learning of it, and tell us at the same time.
7. **Product terms prevail.** The specific terms you are shown for each product (for example, a financial product's contract) apply alongside this policy and may give you more rights, never fewer than the law.
